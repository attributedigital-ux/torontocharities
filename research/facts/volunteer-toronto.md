## NOT claimed

Deliberately left out, and why.

1. **Any count of how many people volunteer in Toronto, or hours contributed.** No primary source was fetched for it. Statistics Canada's giving and volunteering survey was not checked in this session, so no participation figure appears anywhere on the page.
2. **Any claim that volunteering is in decline, or that charities are short of volunteers overall.** The opposite is documented at the organisations checked here: Toronto Humane Society and Toronto Wildlife Centre both have more applicants than places. Generalising from four organisations to the sector would be invention.
3. **Volunteer Toronto's number of listed organisations or positions.** Their homepage was read on 13 Oct 2026 and shows four openings dated that day. A total count was not published there, so none is given.
4. **COSTI Immigrant Services.** Its volunteer page at costi.org/volunteer/ returns 200 but renders almost no readable content without JavaScript, so no programme detail could be verified. Named nowhere on the page. The newcomer and settlement cause is carried by WoodGreen and the YMCA instead.
5. **SickKids, UHN, Michael Garron, ROM and the AGO.** Either their volunteer page could not be reached at a verified URL in this session (SickKids, UHN, ROM) or the charity's own volunteer page was not located (AGO). Hospitals are represented by Sunnybrook alone.
6. **Any processing time for a police record check beyond the single dated TPS figure.** The 13 Oct 2026 backlog is a snapshot, written as one, and no average or typical turnaround is asserted.
7. **Sunnybrook's minimum age for students.** Sunnybrook's own page says 18 across all sites in one place and "minimum 16 years of age" for students in another. Only the explicit all-sites statement is used; the contradiction is not resolved on the page.
8. **Whether a charity currently has openings**, except where its own page states it plainly (Toronto Humane Society closed, Red Door no on-site roles). Openings change weekly and no page asserts availability.
9. **Fred Victor's full volunteer process.** fredvictor.org/volunteer/ redirects to a Volunteer Appreciation Event page rather than a programme page, so only what appears on the individual-volunteer-opportunities page is claimed.
10. **The group fee at The Stop and the programme fee at Daily Bread as amounts.** Both pages say a fee applies without publishing a figure, so the page says a fee applies and gives no number.
