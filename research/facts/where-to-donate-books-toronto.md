## Editor corrections, 13 October 2026

- The draft had attributed row 54's criteria to the canada.ca education
  programs page and had dropped the "with the exception of classic literature"
  qualifier. Both fixed in the draft; rows 40 and 42 to 44 were each correct on
  their own, the draft merged them under one source.
- Victoria's sale runs in September, not October. The `hub_blurb` in the json
  said "their October sales" for Trinity and Victoria together; corrected to
  "their annual sales". The body never carried the error.
- The Victoria page (rows 27 to 31) is JavaScript-rendered and curl returns a
  shell. Its content loads from
  `library.vicu.utoronto.ca/friends/blog/index.php?rest_route=/wp/v2/pages/2413`,
  which curls clean. Add it to the link-check note so the next editor does not
  read the page as empty.

## NOT claimed

- **That books belong in the Toronto blue bin.** The City's accepted-paper list
  (row 50) does not name books, and from 1 January 2026 residential recycling is
  run by Circular Materials, whose site returned 403 to every fetch attempt on
  13 October 2026. The draft sends the reader to Waste Wizard for their own item
  instead of asserting a rule we could not read.
- **That hardcover bindings must be removed before recycling.** Widely repeated
  online, found on no City of Toronto page we could fetch.
- **A count of Little Free Libraries in Toronto.** The organisation publishes a
  world map but no Toronto figure we could read.
- **Anything about Goodwill.** Goodwill Toronto is not in the `charities` table
  and we found no current Toronto book-donation page for it.
- **That any Toronto shelter, hospital or drop-in takes book donations.** No
  Toronto shelter we checked publishes a book-donation page, so none is named.
  Covenant House Toronto's goods page returned 403 on 13 October 2026.
- **Habitat for Humanity GTA ReStore.** Its donation page names furniture and
  appliances and says nothing about books, so it is left out.
- **First Book Canada, ABC Life Literacy Canada, Word-Play and The Reading
  Partnership.** All four are in the database and all four are Toronto literacy
  charities, but none publishes a used-book intake page we could fetch
  (word-play.ca did not resolve at all), so none is named as a drop-off.
- **Tax deductibility of a book donation in general.** Only the four published
  receipt policies in rows 5, 12, 30 and 34 are stated.
- **Numbers of books handled, children served, or dollars raised** beyond rows
  11, 23, 27 and 33, which are each quoted from the organisation's own page.
