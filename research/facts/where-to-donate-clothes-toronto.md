## NOT claimed

- No claim that any one charity is better run, more deserving or more efficient
  than another. The draft describes what each does with a donation and leaves
  the choice to the reader.
- No claim about what share of donated clothing is resold, exported or landfilled
  by any named charity. No charity on this list publishes that figure, and the
  City's report does not break it down by organisation.
- No claim that Value Village pays Diabetes Canada a particular rate per pound.
  The FAQ says only that payment follows the volume delivered.
- No claim that a donor can get a tax receipt for used clothing from any charity
  other than the specific Diabetes Canada statement in row 24 and the Evangel
  Hall Mission and Furniture Bank statements. Receipting rules differ by
  charity and the draft tells the reader to ask.
- No claim about Scott Mission, Covenant House Toronto or Fred Victor clothing
  donations. Their donation pages either returned 403 or did not name a clothing
  intake, so none of them is described here.
- No opening hours for the Goodwill Dundas Street West or Etobicoke Queensway
  community stores beyond the "Opening Fall 2026" and "Opening November 2026"
  labels the location data carries.
- No claim that the 600-box count or the two-thirds split is current. Both are
  dated to the City's 2020 report in the copy.
- No claim that any charity will collect clothing from a Toronto home free of
  charge other than Diabetes Canada, whose own pages describe the service.
