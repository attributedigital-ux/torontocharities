## NOT claimed

- **No period is given for the 4.1 million figure.** Daily Bread's page says
  "last year" without naming the months, and the full Who's Hungry 2025 PDF was
  not opened. The draft attributes the number to Who's Hungry 2025 and says no
  more.
- **No count of soup kitchens or meal programmes in Toronto.** The Toronto
  Drop-In Network figure covers drop-in centres, not meal programmes, and the
  two are not the same thing. The draft uses the TDIN number only for drop-ins.
- **No minimum age for Scott Mission, Fort York Food Bank, The Stop, Parkdale
  Community Food Bank, North York Harvest or Evangel Hall Mission.** None of
  those pages state one. The draft says so rather than guessing.
- **No food handler certificate requirement attributed to any named charity.**
  None of them asks for one on its volunteer page. The draft states the Ontario
  rule and says whose duty it is, and claims nothing about a charity's internal
  policy.
- **No shift times for Fort York Food Bank, Parkdale Community Food Bank, Scott
  Mission or the Yonge Street Mission.** Their pages give none.
- **No fee amount for Daily Bread's Employee Volunteer Program or The Stop's
  group volunteering.** Both say a fee applies; neither publishes the figure.
- **No claim that any charity is currently short of volunteers.** That is a
  Category C judgement and no charity page states it.
- **No wait time for a placement.** Scott Mission says processing time "varies
  depending on the season and the volume of applications"; nobody publishes a
  number.
- **Toronto Vegetarian Food Bank** is in the database but its website
  (tvfb.ca) did not respond on 13 October 2026, so it is not named.
- **Covenant House Toronto** returned HTTP 403 to the fetch, so its volunteer
  page was not read and it is not named.
- **Allan Gardens Food Bank and Churches-on-the-Hill Food Bank** are in the
  database with no website URL and no volunteer page to read, so neither is
  named.

## Flag for the directory

The `food-hunger` category in the `charities` table is mis-populated. A query
joining `charity_categories` to `categories` on `slug='food-hunger'` returned
32 rows on 13 October 2026, all of them environmental charities (Waterfront
Regeneration Trust, Friends of the Rouge Watershed, Oceana Canada and so on).
Daily Bread Food Bank, North York Harvest, Second Harvest and every other food
charity named in this draft were found by name search, not by category. The
`/category/food-hunger/` page this draft links to will therefore show the wrong
charities until the mapping is fixed. Raised here and in
attribute-brain `todo/toronto-charities.md`.
